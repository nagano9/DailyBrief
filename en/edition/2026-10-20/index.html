Summary

PPh 0% tax rule for BUMN restructuring still unenforceable as two implementing regulations remain unsigned

Corporate & SOE Decision-forcing Structural · 30x since 2026-09-20
What changed
As of 19 October 2026, Indonesia's Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) confirmed that the zero-percent final income tax incentive for BUMN asset transfers during restructuring cannot be applied because the required Government Regulation (PP) and Finance Ministry Regulation (PMK) have not been finalized. CNBC Indonesia and ANTARA both reported the gap; Bloomberg Technoz and Kompas.com carried the same confirmation from DJP. [3][6][8][10]
Why it matters
The Danantara restructuring program depends on tax-neutral asset transfers between state entities. Without the PP and PMK, any transfer executed now triggers standard corporate income tax, raising the cost of consolidation and potentially making some mergers commercially unviable at current valuations. The incentive was announced at the policy level but implementation was delegated to two separate regulatory instruments that have not moved.
Second-order effect
The regulatory gap creates a two-speed reform: entities that can afford to wait will delay transfers until the PP and PMK are signed, while those under financial pressure may be forced to transact at full tax cost. This divergence gives well-capitalized BUMN an implicit advantage in the restructuring timeline, potentially concentrating assets in the strongest entities faster than policy intended.
Action
Map every BUMN merger or asset-transfer transaction in the current pipeline against its dependence on the PPh 0% incentive. Identify which transactions have announced timelines that predate probable PP and PMK publication, and flag them for renegotiation or delay. Monitor the Sekretariat Negara gazette and DJP communications for the PP draft.

2

Danantara signals intent to buy BUMN shares as Jakarta Composite Index falls below 5,000

Corporate & SOE Decision-forcing Structural · 30x since 2026-09-20
What changed
On 19 October 2026, Danantara CEO Rosan Roeslani publicly stated that Danantara intends to purchase BUMN shares opportunistically following the Jakarta Composite Index (IHSG) decline below the 5,000 level. CNBC Indonesia and detikFinance both reported the statement on the same date. [2][5][7]
Why it matters
A sovereign wealth vehicle announcing equity purchases at a named index level functions as a price floor signal. It introduces a non-commercial buyer with a political mandate into BUMN equity pricing, which changes the risk calculus for other investors: downside is partially backstopped, but the intervention also signals that market stress has reached a threshold that prompted official response.
Second-order effect
Danantara's buying program, if executed, absorbs free float in already thinly traded BUMN counters. Reduced free float can impair MSCI index eligibility thresholds at a moment when the Bursa Efek Indonesia is scheduled to meet MSCI on 19 October 2026 to discuss index inclusion. The two events in the same week create a structural tension between stabilization and the liquidity requirements that underpin index membership.
Action
Cross-reference Danantara's target BUMN counters against MSCI minimum free-float thresholds. If Danantara buys material stakes in the same week as the BEI-MSCI meeting, assess whether the resulting free-float reduction disqualifies any candidate from inclusion or triggers a review of existing members. Follow the BEI-MSCI meeting outcome before adjusting BUMN equity exposure.

3

Denera withholds PSEL power offtake commitment pending revised RUPTL, exposing project-finance sequencing risk

Energy & Power Decision-forcing Structural · 30x since 2026-09-20
What changed
Bloomberg Technoz reported on 19 October 2026 that Denera is explicitly waiting for the revised RUPTL before confirming power absorption commitments from PSEL (Pengolahan Sampah menjadi Energi Listrik) projects. The revised RUPTL has not been published as of the reporting date. [1][9]
Why it matters
The Danantara lenders panel of 34 financial institutions was assembled to finance PSEL projects. If offtake commitments cannot be confirmed until the RUPTL is revised, and the RUPTL revision is itself pending, the lenders panel has no bankable offtake to lend against. This creates a circular dependency: grid planning must precede offtake commitment, offtake commitment must precede project finance, and project finance must precede construction.
Second-order effect
The RUPTL sequencing bottleneck puts every PSEL project in a holding pattern simultaneously, regardless of how advanced individual projects are in permitting or procurement. The 34-institution lenders panel, assembled at cost and announced publicly, depreciates in value the longer the RUPTL is delayed because financial institutions rotate mandates and credit committees expire approvals. A delay of more than one RUPTL revision cycle could require Danantara to reconstitute the panel.
Action
Identify the current RUPTL revision timeline from Kementerian ESDM and map it against the financial close deadlines of the highest-priority PSEL projects. If the gap exceeds six months, escalate to Danantara and PLN the risk that lender mandates will need renewal, and quantify the reconstitution cost. This analysis should be completed before the next Danantara PSEL coordination meeting.

4

Nscale and Anthropic in talks for a $45 billion AI cloud contract, per Reuters

AI & Frontier Models Emerging Structural · 21x since 2026-09-21
What changed
Reuters reported on 19 October 2026, citing a single source, that Nvidia-backed Nscale and Anthropic are in active negotiations for a new AI cloud supply agreement valued at $45 billion. No contract has been signed and no timeline was given. [4]
Why it matters
A $45 billion committed offtake would be the largest single AI infrastructure contract on record, roughly doubling the scale of deals that have already begun to restructure AI supply chains toward project-finance logic. It continues the thread established when Microsoft withdrew from the Nscale UK deal and investors sold the associated bonds: counterparty selection and credit quality now determine whether AI infrastructure projects can be financed at all.
Second-order effect
If Anthropic becomes the anchor offtake for Nscale at this scale, it shifts competitive pressure onto other hyperscalers and frontier labs to secure equivalent reserved capacity. Labs without long-term compute contracts will face spot-market pricing risk precisely as inference demand scales with model deployment, creating a two-tier market between contracted and uncontracted AI compute consumers.
Action
Assess any AI infrastructure proposal this week against the full project-finance stack: sponsor (Nscale/Nvidia), offtaker (Anthropic), EPC, power provider, grid operator, lender, and guarantor. The Reuters report rests on one unnamed source, so treat the figure as directional rather than confirmed, and watch for corroboration from Anthropic or Nscale official channels before adjusting position sizing.
